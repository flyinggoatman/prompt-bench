Saved setups

Keep a combination you liked: the master, the ticked modules, the dials and the text boxes. Setups are stored on this device only, and never include your cast or photos. Modules are remembered by their wording rather than their number, so a setup still loads correctly after you add or remove packs.

Working notes

Generate one image at a time. Reject anything with a spelling error rather than accepting it, since a rerun usually fixes it. Rejected images stay in the conversation and influence the next one, so when a detail sticks, edit your earlier message to branch rather than replying again. After roughly five images in one chat, start a fresh one and re-attach the reference photos.
